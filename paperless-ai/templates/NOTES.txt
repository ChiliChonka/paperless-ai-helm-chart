{{- else }}

- Paperless-ngx >= 3.5 only accepts the host of its PAPERLESS_URL and localhost. If paperless-ai
  uses another host name (e.g. the internal Service), add it to PAPERLESS_ALLOWED_HOSTS of
  Paperless-ngx – otherwise the connection test fails with "Bad Request (400)".

UPGRADE FROM 3.x (chart 0.1.x)
- Back up the volume first. On the first start data/.env and documents.db are migrated to
  data/config.json and data/paperless-ai.db; the old files stay untouched.
- Check the log: kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs statefulset/{{ include "paperless-ai.fullname" . }}

Single replica only: SQLite and the scan schedule do not support more than one pod.
